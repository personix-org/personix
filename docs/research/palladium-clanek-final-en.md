# Irresponsibility is free of charge

As you have probably noticed world states' relationships are worsening, individual states' elected representatives are not able to bring a solution to more and more discontented voters. It is happening despite the fact that we live in the richest possible times, the third world included. It is, on one side, a fascinating era, but looking back at history it opens up a lot of worries and fears.  

### It is what it is and we are where we are.

In the following lines I would like to make a pretty descriptive summary of where I see the problem and why in the form of A leads to B and B leads to C in order to see the logic in the contemporary situation. Later it will help us to identify key aspects that should be fixed in one of the possible solutions. (The author draws examples mainly from Central Europe and the European Union (EU)).
 
### Economic view

Anarcho-capitalists often describe the following economic spiral of deterioration. General election right brought a temptation for various social-level members to push elected politicians towards their needs, i.e. lobbying laws to limit competition for the entrepreneurs, obtaining higher government officers' salaries or retirees' pensions, etc. - preferably using other people's money. Politicians with an incentive to be elected again, driven by their economic needs and self-satisfying high-power position in administrations hear the whispered words "support", "reelect", "solidarity" in their brain and often against all their own inner conviction they set the course toward increasingly socialist policies, because they are asked to. This is what they can do.

### Secondary non-economic view

What they cannot do is to bring a solution for non-economic calls from voting people, i.e. losing economic strength for the middle class with Western world deindustrialization towards Asia, increased illegal migration and increasing criminality, increased entropy in the information sphere, etc. Promises and easy solutions fail in such cases rapidly. Politicians are turning towards regulations, utopia projects (the [Green Deal](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/priorities-2024-2029/european-green-deal_en), EU), restrictions and totalitarian tendencies ([Chat Controls 1.0 and prepared 2.0](https://www.patrick-breyer.de/en/posts/chat-control/), VPN restrictions and end-to-end cryptography restrictions in EU, [Flock surveillance in US](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/12/effs-investigations-expose-flock-safetys-surveillance-abuses-2025-review)). 

Maybe you ask yourself: Why the hell on earth is it possible that EU/USA democracy can turn towards totality? Not many educated people can observe the difference between the words totality and dictatorship. Totality is controlling every major aspect of people's lives from work assignments to family child-raising policies. The dictatorship rules the people by power, i.e. probably not free elections, but also no need to drive every aspect of citizens' lives.

### Non-economics issues ends-up in economy again

Each of the above-mentioned state interventions has its costs. A new form is made, a new centralized data collection database is created, new rule-enforcement units are formed. Material, operation cost and human resources are increasing. Almost no agenda is given up by the states, they are mostly modernizing for more effectivity when circumstances force them to do so. Saved money is never invested back in the form of decreased taxation.

Generally we have a new agenda and a growing institution and the easiest way to cover such a naive way to implement all the new stuff is to allocate more taxes from taxpayers who are subjects of the regulations and restrictions. More regulations and restrictions mean more time spent on their fulfilment by the subjects of these restrictions, and we are those subjects, less time for core business, core social network (family, friends) and even disillusionment and loss of creative enthusiasm.

### Escaping consequence

But what is increasing is our thoughts on how to avoid the codified rules. The easiest is to avoid paying part of taxes (based on work taxation) and join the gray economics, more serious are bribery and pure crime. At this point it must be said that the more work taxation avoidance is reached, the more rapidly property taxation will come into place in the next years).

We are losing the sense of boundary between useful rules and the useless ones - this is harmful.

Let's think for a moment about the people changing the world. They are not just the Silicon Valley masterminds, they are ordinary entrepreneurs building delivery parcel boxes for families to help them save some energy and time in various places, farmers who have been cultivating the same fields for generations, etc. The creative motivated minority today is a pure minority in general (parliamentary) elections. Why is it? They are everywhere, so why is it possible? Because they allowed themselves to become corrupted by the system and now they have no way out - farmers obtaining entitlement-based land subsidies, industrial businessmen deferring their development ideas till they obtain government support, highly taxed lower-middle class and poor people gladly taking any government support for everyday living.

**It is free of charge to stay like that and be irresponsible for yourself.** It costs you a lot and you can lose a lot when you are fighting everyone to find the way out (opt-out not possible).

If you want to reach "something more" the natural way is to realize that the low hanging fruit in the vertical pyramid of power is the ideal target. You can find there easy high-income positions if you just have a developed soft-skill (nothing more often needed). Secondly you can try lobbying or corruption through money or counter-service promises as a rational choice. Win-win for briber and bribed person in general (except that you are susceptible to blackmail from that time). For the wealthy it is not that difficult to support somebody loyal into the vertical pyramid of power in elections - yes, here comes the oligarchy.

After some time institutional actors adopt the mission disruption as a normal status. Why provide services for a standard salary when there are multiple possibilities to let taxpaying clients pay more as an extra service. The vulnerable vertical pyramid of power allowed that, it just set up incentives and the system evolved accordingly. It would be silly to think it will end otherwise. I personally call the target asymptote "institutional laziness".

No institution is spared. But I have to say: honor to the individuals who perform their duties honorably.

### Society adhesive detachment

By far the worst consequences of institutional laziness we can observe in justice systems. I set aside the differences between Continental European and/or Anglo-American justice systems, in the end the difference brings no different incentives. To explain what is wrong let me start with the strong assumption - we individuals need to generally feel as a part of single and/or multiple communities (family, friends, business, neighborhood, ...). 

Please do not mix the concept of social network and community. Social Network is the set of your personal links and contacts and it is limited enumerable. Within your social networks you can sort people into several communities (some people ends-up in multiple communities). The community as a whole is probably far greater that what you see in your social network boundaries. General communities mostly do not have sharp boundaries, have overlaps and many interesting observable properties.

The community members often like to be part of them and the community expects members to participate in the community's common goals. In fact you are a member of a tribe and you feel terrible when you do everything right but the community betrays you. The modern state (dating approximately from 1950 from Bismark Germany and later) is artificially filling in the role of communities. The real communities had all the power to ostracize, penalize and even punish their members and defend their members against all outer threats from members of other tribes. This mechanism evolved over hundreds of thousands of years for homo-sapiens. You can call it tribalism, but you cannot erase it from society, because it is with us and will stay with us for a very long time. Any social arrangement has to take it into account to avoid making an empty ideology based upon hatred and the denial of human properties and often ending up in bloodbath. The whole 20th century was full of such attempts.

This is not folk psychology. The need to belong in community is a [fundamental human motivation](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.117.3.497), social rejection activates the same brain regions as [physical pain](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1089134), and prolonged isolation carries a [mortality risk comparable to smoking](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614568352).

The sense of justice fulfilled among community members has probably a different sensed boundary than set via laws and institutionalized justice.

I can give you a real life example from Central Europe. You as an owner-builder have to comply with many regulations during the building process that are audited by government institutions (e.g. minimal insulation, roof shape, minimal distance from neighbor land, electricity installation safety certificate, etc.) - some are useful because you need them for your own good anyway and some only increase the building's overall cost. When something goes wrong you may wrongly expect that you can use those documents to prove something and push things towards a faster resolution. What a naive expectation.

As a tenant within any building, when you are for example injured by electricity which the landlord did not take care of, you have to collect evidence by yourself (for some evidence collecting, you need the landlord's permission). You cannot just reference a missing electricity safety certificate, a receipt of your hospitalization and the problem is solved. The procedure is much more complicated and you pay for it all. When the court is held and you win the lawsuit against the opposing party and it has to pay you satisfaction based upon your spending bills, they are confronted against standardized allowances and mostly reduced. The reality of 2026: you obtain as compensation approximately 1/6 of the real justice process spending (compensation for legal services set out in those compensation tables does not correlate at all with market realities).

What I am trying to say is that for smaller transgressions, there is no incentive to defend yourself, because you are just losing more money and doing yourself more harm. The justice institutions are just deliberately, via elected parliament members, pushing the efficient limit for an affordable lawsuit even higher. The motivation is to reduce justice system load. Nobody responsible works on higher efficiency to process more cases. It is the same institutional incentive I mentioned above - they are solving their problems preferably over the taxpaying clients' problems.

**As a result, for me as an arbitrary actor I have no incentive then to behave honestly towards other society actors - it is free of charge a second time to be irresponsible.** The society cohesion rots because people feel abandoned by the surrogate community - i.e. the state in this case - they do not feel a part of it.

### "Easy" escape

Where there is demand, there sooner or later supply appears - a surrogate community. What if I offer you an artificially tailored community which will offer you nothing but wrap it in shiny packaging. The "nothing" can be conspiracies, alternative extreme ways of living, churches, part of society against the other part, etc. They could also offer promising recipes from past times. The shine is the tight link to community - it will never abandon you, they chain you via warm greetings and keeping you theirs, their business is built on top of that simple rule. The calling for a surrogate community is so strong that you can deny yourself and your own experiences of the world in order to be part of the community.

Do you recognize it in today's world? Your brain could have served you MAGA, or the Brexit Leave campaign a few years ago as a first association. We weakened our communities and let defectors even work against us (the previous community) - what a waste of valuable human resources already speaking your own language, knowing your cultural background, etc.

That was it. The description of where we are. Let me from now on make a couple of thoughts on how the possible fix can look.

### An individual's freedom ends where another's begins.

As a secondary school pupil I first learned a lot about the principle: an individual's freedom ends where another's begins. It is not one of my favorite freedom definitions, but we use it for the following reasoning line. My strong claim is that this definition's consistent application outside commonly accepted freedoms like the freedoms of speech, of movement, of ownership etc. is the key to fixing the society relationships' natural hierarchy. Moreover it leads to one point where irresponsibility will become expensive, really expensive.

When I said outside commonly understood freedoms I did mean mainly reporting the important information of other community members' behaviour and judging them. This is a pretty controversial claim because these are the main kinds of information and behaviour that are restricted by states. Ordinary people do not have the privilege to do so, for ages it is the exclusive right of state institutions and representatives. We are allowed to do so only via various different kinds of lawsuits and dedicated justice institutions (with all the institutional laziness described above). Now you have an obvious temptation to call me a totalitarian, because it was always the totalitarian establishment happily producing any kind of list of people and what they did (e.g. current [Communist China's social credit system](https://merics.org/en/comment/chinas-social-credit-score-untangling-myth-reality)). The devil is in the details.

People nowadays happily collect information about others and judge them based on the provided information of various origin. It is happening across small communities on a small scale. Rumors and gossip are the main types of them. Do the listeners take such information into account in their decision-making? Generally yes - happily. Do they judge and adjust behaviour towards the subjects of the rumors and gossip? Generally again yes. What is preventing people from doing that? The reputation of the provider of such information and in rare moments the reputation of the source (if somebody has the motivation to ask for it). Also, when I spread false accusations the community sooner or later turns against myself. Or I find myself in a precarious situation where it is much more efficient to apologize or compensate for the harm I made. This is the feedback driving you within acceptable boundaries by the community. People are eager for information, especially negative ones, but it has its limits - i.e. feedback from the community you want to stay in. It is a fully spontaneous decentralized mechanism of information spreading despite the fact that communities often have centralized organization.

What is the result of this? People feel responsibility for their behaviour and even their spoken word, because they want to stay part of the community. If not, they rather leave, find other communities (changing jobs, sportclubs, places of living, ...). People then feel at least some respect from the other community members and feel a duty to fight for members of the community in certain situations and other community members see it - reputation is gained then - slowly by time, energy and even some financial or material spending. The irresponsibility is now expensive, very expensive. The drop of reputation can be much faster than the slow and expensive gaining of it.

At this place you can list all kinds of community fails towards members and vice-versa. I maintain that these are not failures, but that the community or member are not worth each other. 

### Equilibrium is not enough, we need certain stability

Unlike anarcho-capitalists I believe they are missing something. They are talking about equilibrium coming together with an absolutely free market and indisputable ownership - basically yes, I agree. But the equilibrium they describe is quite full of dynamic changes - i.e. people changing communities because with any additional economic interest you are leaving a community and joining others. The society is too granular, unclear for an observer. It seems like a pretty dynamic system.

I am talking about the system with a stable equilibrium we need. Driven not just by economic interests but also by true responsibility coming from the community's feedback. We need a tool that allows communities to change not rapidly (they must have a consensus mechanism) as a symbol of instability (we see it now in the current world) but dynamically, still reacting to outer and inner conditions - more than we see with rigid state systems nowadays.

You can argue that the reputation measures can bring free market services, so it is still ancap. But on the other hand you can call today's state system and ancap micro-states from a statistical point of view - and we do not call it that. In fact we need to research such a reputation system much more in order to find out whether some known system names (e.g. meritocracy) fit it as a consequential result.

This is the mission of [personix.org](https://personix.org), which is designing a new communication protocol for a reputation network consisting of decentralized identities (a different concept than [W3C DID](https://www.w3.org/TR/did-core/)). First publications show it could be uncensorable, incorruptible and implementable with today's technologies. The team around Personix is preparing a social simulation to prove their early thesis, in order to let the public disrupt it. We will see in the near future what the outcomes would be.

I started with the apocalyptic state of today's world and drew a much worse one if we do not act. This is because irresponsibility is free of charge. My appeal is to engineer a system where irresponsibility is expensive - through earned reputation and decentralization.
